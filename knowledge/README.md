# Stebula knowledge base

Everything we know, decided, and plan for Stebula, in one place. Start here.

Stebula (formerly Threshold) is an AI agent you can talk to while watching its processes work: what it attends to, how its modeled signals shift, how its self-model and personality form, and where each piece came from. Every visual comes from recorded data.

## How this stays current

- Every change to Stebula adds an entry to the decision log and updates roadmap status.
- Every new finding goes in the Findings registry (data/findings.json) and gets a human comparison.
- References stay marked "verify" until checked against the original source.
