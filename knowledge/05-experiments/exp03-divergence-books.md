# Experiment 03: Do experiences shape its personality?

Status: SPECCED (prompt 2).

## Design
1. Reset both agents.
2. Agent A reads Peter Rabbit (disobedience, risk, consequence).
3. Agent B reads The Velveteen Rabbit (love, becoming real).
4. Fresh baseline: empty memory, same personality questions.
5. Compare trait sets, strengths, provenance, and behavioral proxies.

Use public domain texts only for anything published (gutenberg.org).

## Interpretation
- Traits diverge in the direction of the books, and the baseline lacks them: experience shaped the profile.
- Both agents and the baseline show the same traits: they come from the base model, not the reading.
- Traits stated but not shown in behavior: self-description without substance.

## Human comparison
Narrative transportation and the influence of stories on self-concept; socialization research. Key difference: Stebula's inputs are fully controlled and its baseline is a literal copy.
