# Glossary

- **Affect (modeled):** surprise, conflict, curiosity. Numbers computed from the conversation that change which thoughts win. Not feelings.
- **Agent A / B:** two personas. A reasons as an empiricist, B by analogy. Same underlying model.
- **Audit:** a separate model call that reviews recent turns for contradictions, sycophancy, overclaiming, and memory errors.
- **Baseline:** a copy with empty memory, used to tell experience-driven traits from base-model traits.
- **Brain view:** the live map of Stebula's software processes. Not a scan of the model.
- **Calibration gap:** stated confidence minus measured accuracy.
- **Decoy rule:** the narrow rule the starting examples also fit in the hidden-rule task.
- **Digest:** reading material to Stebula passage by passage.
- **Episodic memory:** a stored record of one experience (a passage or a conversation turn) with its source.
- **Hidden-rule task:** a guessing game where only tests you expect to fail can reveal the real rule.
- **Indicator:** a property a consciousness theory says a conscious system should have.
- **Provenance:** the exact events and quotes that built or weakened a trait.
- **Said vs shown:** comparing a trait Stebula claims with measured behavior.
- **Self-model:** what Stebula believes about itself, what it is unsure of, and what it wants to explore.
- **Self-prediction test:** Stebula predicts a fresh copy's answer, two fresh copies answer blind, code scores it.
- **Self-report:** any claim about its own inner states. Logged as data, not evidence.
- **Workspace:** the limited space (2 slots) that thoughts compete for. Only winners reach the answer.
