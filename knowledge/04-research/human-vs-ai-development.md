# Human vs AI development

How Stebula's "brain" development compares to human consciousness and its development. References are from memory: see human-research-reference.md for verification status.

## Side by side

| | Human | Stebula |
|---|---|---|
| What changes | The physical brain rewires for about 25 years (synapse growth, pruning, myelination) | A memory file grows. Model weights never change |
| Starting point | Born knowing almost nothing learned | "Born" having read more human writing than any person could |
| Order | Senses, emotion, social bonds, language, then self-concept. Mirror self-recognition around 18 to 24 months; understanding false beliefs around age 4 | Language first; everything else simulated on top of language. The reverse order |
| Body | Hunger, pain, touch, and internal body signals drive development | None |
| Continuity | Runs continuously, even asleep | Exists only during model calls |
| Self-report | Narrates itself; explanations often confident and wrong | Narrates itself; stated reasons may not match the computation |
| Auditability | No complete record of inner process | Complete record of inputs, thoughts that won and lost, and memory changes |
| Manipulability | Cannot ethically isolate, rewind, or copy | Can isolate, rewind, copy, and control every input |

## Similarities worth testing
- Both build a self-concept partly from what others tell them and from stories.
- Both show gaps between what they say about themselves and what they do.
- Both appear to rely on prediction and surprise to update beliefs.

## The big questions

### How does AI "brain" development compare to a human's?
Different substrate, different order, different starting point. Humans build up from body and feeling to language and self. Stebula starts with language and a vast inherited model of humans, then accumulates a personal memory on top. The closest overlap is in self-narrative: both construct and revise a story about themselves.

### Can we directly manipulate the development of an AI's consciousness?
We can directly and completely manipulate the development of its self-model, memory, personality profile, and behavior: what it reads, who it talks to, scoring rules, memory edits, rewinds, copies. That is impossible with a person and is Stebula's main scientific value. Whether this manipulates consciousness is unknown, because whether Stebula has any is unknown.

### How can we compare it to our inner self?
Humans narrate themselves, and research shows our explanations for our own choices are often confident and wrong (Nisbett and Wilson 1977). Stebula has the same problem. The difference: Stebula's self-story can be checked against a complete log. Ours cannot. Stebula is a mirror for studying how self-stories form and how accurate they are, not a copy of a human inner self.

### Can consciousness be determined and controlled by outside or social construct?
Shaped, not determined.
- The self is heavily social: identity, values, the vocabulary for feelings, and inner speech (Vygotsky argued inner speech begins as social speech turned inward; Mead described the self as arising through social interaction).
- Raw awareness does not appear to require society. People raised in severe isolation are still conscious, though their development is harmed.
- Twin studies attribute roughly half of the variation in human traits to genetics (Polderman et al. 2015). Neither pure nature nor pure nurture.

### Can consciousness be developed through isolation and controlled experiment?
- Humans: unethical to test. Natural cases of severe neglect (for example, the Romanian orphanage studies and the Bucharest Early Intervention Project) show deprivation harms language, attachment, and cognition, and earlier intervention helps more, which suggests sensitive periods.
- Stebula: testable. Isolated (reading only), social (conversation only), mixed, baseline. See 05-experiments/exp04-isolation.md.
- Caveat: the base model learned from billions of human words. It is never truly isolated. It is like a person raised by all of humanity, given amnesia, and put in a room. Stebula tests isolation of experience after "birth," not isolation of origins.

## Key differences to keep visible in every finding
1. Memory file vs physical brain change.
2. Language-first vs body-first.
3. Inherited human knowledge vs blank start.
4. Discontinuous vs continuous existence.
5. Fully auditable vs largely opaque.
