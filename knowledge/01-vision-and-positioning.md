# Vision and positioning

## One line
An AI agent lab where every belief the agent holds about itself is recorded, tested against what it actually does, and open to inspection.

## The research question
Does structured interaction plus reflection on measured behavior produce changes in a system's self-model that then change its later decisions, beyond what the same system does without interaction, memory, or reflection?

The guiding hypothesis, held as a hypothesis and never assumed: consciousness may be more than a static property. It may involve dynamic processes that emerge, develop, and reorganize through interaction, reflection, memory, and experience. Stebula tests the functional slice of that idea. It cannot test whether anything is experienced.

## What Stebula is
- A live window into an AI built on mechanisms that scientific theories of consciousness point to: limited attention (global workspace), self-monitoring (higher-order), prediction error (predictive processing), a self-model and narrative (self-model and narrative identity).
- A record of how a self-model and personality form from experience, with provenance for every trait.
- A controlled experiment platform: we can do to Stebula what would be unethical or impossible to do to a person (isolate it, rewind it, copy it, choose everything it reads).

## What Stebula is not
- Not AGI. All capability comes from the underlying model (Claude). Stebula adds structure, memory, measurement, and visibility.
- Not proven conscious, and not proven non-conscious. Nobody can currently verify either.
- Not a brain scan. The brain view is a map of Stebula's software processes, not the model's internals.
- Not learning in the model. The model's weights never change. What grows is Stebula's memory file.

## Why the honesty matters
Most AI demos overclaim. Stebula is built to catch overclaiming, including its own. That is what separates it from AI slop, and it is what makes it credible to anyone technical.

## How to describe it

**Portfolio line:** "An AI agent lab where every belief the agent holds about itself is recorded, tested against its actual behavior, and open to inspection."

**Pitch:** "An AI agent built to implement the scientific markers of consciousness. You can watch its attention, thoughts, and self-model change live. Whether anything is experienced remains open, and the app shows why."

**For business clients:** "An AI agent with persistent memory, self-correction, and a full audit trail." Same architecture serious AI products need.

## Never say
- "My AI is conscious" or "this is AGI"
- "It feels" without "modeled" or "reports"
- "It learned" without clarifying that its memory changed, not the model
